# 👥 BLIGHTBORNE: NPC REGISTRY

---

## 👹 HOSTILE FACTIONS & VILLAINS

### 1. Overseer Vhol (Advanced-Rank Centurion) — `💀 DECEASED`
* **Role:** Former Commander of Fort Kar-Vash, in charge of Sector 09's slave extraction and transit cages.
* **Appearance:** 9-foot-tall, four-armed demon in spiked dread-plate with a back-mounted pitch boiler.
* **Fate:** Slain by Rashuk in the Amphitheater of Bone during Session 1. His **Obsidian Greatcleaver** was claimed by Rashuk.

### 2. Captain Krug (Intermediate-Rank Brute) — `💀 DECEASED`
* **Role:** Vanguard tracker captain of the Iron Fang Syndicate.
* **Fate:** Ambushed and decapitated by Rashuk in Dead-Water Gulch during Act 1.

### 3. The Masked Grand Inquisitor (Saint-Rank Warlord) — `🟢 ACTIVE / UNMET`
* **Role:** Supreme Commander of the Sovereign Inquisition in Zul-Gaza.
* **Significance:** Ordered the total extermination and crucifixion of Sanctuary 15 years ago.
* **Crest:** *A Crown of Bone Piercing a Weeping Eye*.

## 👥 CASUALTIES OF FORT KAR-VASH

### 1. Old Geth (One-Eyed Goblin Apothecary) — `💀 DECEASED`
* **Role:** Former slave infirmary doctor in Fort Kar-Vash.
* **Fate:** Following Vhol's death, the fortress garrison blamed the slave quarters and massacred Geth along with all human captives in the infirmary.
* **Legacy:** Gifted Rashuk the *Alchemical Quenching Dust* and *Ghost-Poppy Salve* before his demise.

### 2. Elia (10-Year-Old Human Captive) — `💀 DECEASED`
* **Role:** Former child captive chained to the magma crosses.
* **Fate:** Executed in the post-battle retaliation sweep by demon sentries. Her death triggered Rashuk's complete descent into **Lawful Evil** vengeance.
