# 👥 BLIGHTBORNE: NPC REGISTRY

---

## 👹 HOSTILE FACTIONS & VILLAINS

### 1. Overseer Vhol (Advanced-Rank Centurion)
* **Role:** Commander of Fort Kar-Vash, in charge of Sector 09's slave extraction and mana consecration.
* **Appearance:** 9-foot-tall, four-armed demon in spiked dread-plate with a back-mounted pitch boiler.
* **Personality:** Arrogant, sadistic, ambitious. Craves noble recognition from Grand Inquisitor Gortash.
* **Phase 2 Shift:** Goes into a manic frenzy upon smelling Rashuk's forbidden red blood.
* **Combat Stats:** CR 5 | AC 17 | HP 85 | 2x Cleaver +7 (2d8+4), Pitch Spray (3d6F+1d6A), Centurion Buckler Reaction (+2 AC).

### 2. Captain Krug (Intermediate-Rank Brute)
* **Role:** Field tracker and vanguard captain of the Iron Fang Syndicate.
* **Appearance:** Four-tusked orcish demon in rusted lamellar iron armor wielding a heavy notched cleaver.
* **Status:** Slain in Dead-Water Gulch during Act 1.

### 3. Grand Inquisitor Gortash (Saint-Rank Warlord)
* **Role:** Supreme Commander of the Imperial Inquisition in Zul-Gaza, architect of the Aethel-Engine.
* **Significance:** Ordered the total extermination and crucifixion of Sanctuary 15 years ago.
* **Crest:** *A Crown of Bone Piercing a Weeping Eye*.

## 🤝 ALLIES & SURVIVORS

### 1. Old Geth (Demon Apothecary)
* **Role:** Slave infirmary doctor in Fort Kar-Vash.
* **Appearance:** Aging, hunchbacked goblin-demon with peeling gray scales and a blind cataract eye.
* **Philosophy:** Secretly despises the Hegemony's cruelty. Provides painkilling salves to dying human captives.
* **Alliance:** Gives Rashuk *Alchemical Quenching Dust* and helps convert Fort Kar-Vash into a secret resistance sanctuary.

### 2. Elia (10-Year-Old Human Captive)
* **Role:** Rescued human child chained to the central cross over the magma pit.
* **Significance:** Represents the mirror of Rashuk at age 10 when his sanctuary was destroyed.
* **Relationship:** Deeply devoted to the "Crimson Demon" who caught her falling cross.
