⚖️ DM Rules & Difficulty Cheat-Sheet 2-Second Glance
🎯 Table Difficulty DCs

DC 10 (Easy): Spot obvious tracks, scale dry wooden wall.

DC 12 (Moderate): Conceal red blood in mud, resist hound knock-down.

DC 14 (Hard): Evade Blood-Dowsing Braziers, dodge Vhol's Boiling Pitch Jet.

DC 16 (Very Hard): Sever winch chain while balancing on magma rim.

💥 Combat & Crits

Brutal Crits: Max Base Dmg + 1 Normal Roll + Modifiers.

Intent to Attack: Initiative is rolled immediately before any declared attack resolves.

Ability Checks in Combat: Consumes full Action.

Cover: In Cover (+2 AC) | Total Cover (Un-targetable).

💀 0 HP & Death Saves

DC 10 Save: Success = stabilize with 1 HP (Prone). Failure = 1 Strike.

2 Strikes = Permanent Death.

Damage while at 0 HP: Taking damage 2 separate times = instant permanent death.
