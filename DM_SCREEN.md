## ⚡ 0. HOMEBREW COMPENDIUM & MASTER TABLE RULES

### 1. Combat Initiation & Surprise
* **Zero Free Pre-Combat Hits:** The moment any player or NPC declares intent to attack (*"I stab him," "I fire an arrow"*), **EVERYONE rolls Initiative immediately BEFORE the attack resolves**.
* **Attacking from Hidden:** If successfully hidden (Stealth vs Passive Perception), target gains the **Surprised Condition** (skips Round 1 turn), and attacker gains **Advantage** on their opening attack.
* **Ability Checks in Combat:** Active checks in combat (Perception, Insight, Arcana, Investigation) consume your **FULL ACTION**.

### 2. The Help System & Help Tokens
* Each PC has **2 Help Tokens** maximum.
* A player can spend 1 Help Token (within 30 ft) to grant a **+1d6 Advantage Die** to an ally's d20 roll **ONLY IF they provide a valid narrative explanation of HOW they actively help**.

### 3. 0 HP, Dying & Death Saves (2-Strike Lethal System)
* Drop to 0 HP = Fall Prone and Unconscious (Dying State).
* On each turn while dying, roll a **DC 10 Death Saving Throw (d20)**:
  * **10+ (Success):** Stabilize and wake up with **1 HP** (remains Prone)!
  * **1–9 (Failure):** 1 Death Strike. **💥 2 FAILURES = PERMANENT DEATH!**
  * **Nat 20:** Instantly regain 1 HP and stand up immediately.
* **Damage while at 0 HP:** Taking damage **2 separate times** before healing = instant permanent death.

### 4. Brutal Critical Hits
* On a **Natural 20**, attacks deal **Maximum Base Damage + 1 Normal Damage Roll + Ability Modifiers** (e.g., 1d6 weapon crit = `6 + 1d6 + modifier`).

### 5. Simplified 2-Second Cover Rule
* **🧱 In Cover (+2 AC):** Behind barrels, low walls, thick pillars, or standing behind an ally/enemy.
* **🚫 Total Cover:** 100% concealed behind solid stone wall or iron door—cannot be targeted.

### 6. Blight-Born Blood Hazard (Setting Homebrew)
* Hybrids are an illegal heresy in the Demon Hegemony. Rashuk's blood is vibrant crimson human red.
* When taking heavy slashing/piercing damage or near Blood-Dowsing Braziers, a **DC 12–14 check** is required to conceal or extinguish the tell before demon sentries sound the extermination alarm.

### 7. Simplified 2-Coin Economy
* **10 Silver Pieces (SP) = 1 Gold Piece (GP)**. No copper pieces exist.

## 🗺️ 1. SESSION 1: THE ASHEN TRAIL (ACT BREAKDOWN)

```
[ ACT 1: THE BLOODHOUND HUNT ] ➔ [ ACT 2: FORT KAR-VASH ] ➔ [ ACT 3: AMPHITHEATER OF BONE ] ➔ [ ACT 4: THE HORIZON ]
 (Midnight Chase & Gortash)        (Infiltration & Geth)      (Boss: Overseer Vhol CR 5)     (Moral Choice & Intel)
```

### Act 1: The Bloodhound Hunt (0:00 – 0:45)
* **Setup:** Rashuk is hunted through the **Rot-Wood Bog** by the **Iron Fang Pack** after decapitating a sadistic convoy commander who was about to burn a 10-year-old human slave boy.
* **Combat (Dead-Water Gulch):** 2x Bloodhound Chimeras, 1x Captain Krug, 2x Iron Fang Scouts.
* **The Red Blood Risk:** DC 12 Sleight of Hand / Athletics to stomp the mud and wipe away crimson blood before Krug dies.
* **The Revelation:** Prying open the commander's brass satchel reveals Directive 409 bearing *A Crown of Bone Piercing a Weeping Eye*, confirming the name: **Grand Inquisitor Gortash**.

### Act 2: The Salt-Rot Citadel (0:45 – 1:45)
* **Setup:** Infiltrating **Fort Kar-Vash**, a black basalt fortress over a sulfur rift.
* **Hazard:** Emerald **Blood-Dowsing Braziers** flare violet-crimson near human blood. Sneak through rafters (DC 13 Acrobatics) or douse flue chains (DC 13 Sleight of Hand).
* **Archives:** Reading the 15-year-old Sanctuary purge ledger signed by Gortash.
* **Ally Roleplay (Old Geth):** One-eyed goblin apothecary in the slave infirmary. Gives **Alchemical Quenching Dust** and warns: *"Power without purpose makes you no different than Gortash."*

### Act 3: The Amphitheater of Bone (1:45 – 3:15)
* **Arena:** Circular volcanic obsidian ring over a bubbling magma pit. Three crosses suspended on chains holding captives (including 10yo Elia).
* **Boss:** **Overseer Vhol** (CR 5 Advanced-Rank Centurion — AC 17, HP 85, 4 Arms, Cleaver +7, Boiling Pitch Spray).
* **Winch Countdown:** Every 2 rounds on Initiative 20, winch lowers crosses 5 ft closer to magma (AC 14, 15 HP to lock/sever).
* **Phase 2 (Under 42 HP):** Vhol smells Rashuk's red blood, realizing he is the Blight-Born survivor of Sanctuary. Gains +1 attack/round in a manic bounty frenzy.

### Act 4: The Moral Crossroads & The Horizon (3:15 – 3:45)
* **Climax:** Vhol defeated, Elia pulled from the magma rim.
* **Three Moral Paths:**
  * 🩸 **Demonic Wrath (Leans Evil):** Sadistically butcher Vhol, terrify captives into submission.
  * 🛡️ **The Ashen Shield (Leans Good):** Clean execution, tend Elia's burns: *"As long as I live, no one burns today."*
  * ⚖️ **Lawful Pragmatist (True Neutral):** Execute Vhol for silence, seize signet ring, commandeer transport lines.
* **Campaign Rewards:** Inquisitorial Signet Ring, Grand Tithe Route Map to **Zul-Gaza**, and Fort Kar-Vash safehouse.

## 👹 2. MONSTER & BOSS DIRECTORY

### Overseer Vhol (CR 5 Advanced Boss — 85 Total HP)
* **Phase 1: The Spiked Centurion (45 HP | AC 17 | Speed 30 ft | Init +2)**
  * **Attacks:** 2x Greatcleaver +7 (`2d8+4` slash + DC 14 Str push 10 ft), Boiling Pitch Spray (Recharge 5-6, 15-ft cone, DC 14 Dex or `3d6` fire + `1d6` acid), Centurion Buckler Reaction (+2 AC).
  * **Battlefield Action 1 — Tectonic Magma-Cleave:** *Tell:* Cleaver glows white-hot; pivots to face last damager. *Counterplay:* Tank strikes last to redirect cone, or move outside 20 ft. *Resolve:* 20-ft cone: DC 14 Dex save or `3d10` fire/slashing and knocked Prone.
  * **Battlefield Action 2 — Winch of the Damned:** *Tell:* Locks gear; winch begins lowering 3 crosses 5 ft. *Counterplay:* Deal 15 damage to winch cable (AC 14) or DC 13 Athletics to jam cog. *Resolve:* Crosses lower 5 ft; captives take `1d6` heat damage.
* **Phase 2: Sanguine Blood Frenzy (40 HP | AC 17 | Speed 35 ft | Init +2)**
  * *Trigger:* Discards buckler upon losing 45 HP or confirming Rashuk's forbidden crimson blood.
  * **Attacks:** Frenzied Multiattack: 2x Cleavers +7 (`2d8+4` slash) + 1x Pitch Claw +7 (`1d10+4` pierce + `1d6` acid).
  * **Battlefield Action 1 — Slag Eruption:** *Tell:* Slams cleaver into magma rim; cracks snake toward party. *Counterplay:* Move 25+ ft away or leap onto perimeter posts. *Resolve:* 20-ft burst: DC 14 Dex save or `3d10` fire/bludgeoning.
  * **Battlefield Action 2 — Imperial Soul-Siphon:** *Tell:* Channels regional spire; emerald soul-tendrils latch onto targets. *Counterplay:* Break Concentration (DC 10+ Con save on damage) or break LoS. *Resolve:* DC 14 Con save or `3d10` necrotic damage and Vhol heals for half.

## 🌲 3. DEEP BIOME & LOCATION PROFILES

### 1. The Rot-Wood Bog
* **Sensory Atmosphere:** Black acidic rain stinging bare flesh; petrified ironwood trees weeping gray lichen; sulfur-soaked knee-deep muck.
* **Hazards:** Exposed vision capped at 60 ft (ranged Disadvantage beyond 60 ft); deep slurry costs 2x movement speed; climbing petrified snags requires DC 12 Athletics/Acrobatics (failure by 5+ deals 1d6 piercing).
* **Cover:** Hollowed ironwood trunks grant Total Cover; steam vents grant Heavily Obscured (+2 AC, Disadvantage on incoming attacks, DC 11 Con save or 1d4 poison).

### 2. The Dead-Water Gulch
* **Chasm Mechanics:** 20-ft basalt trench with 15-ft sheer walls; collapsed bridge creates bottleneck; high ridge grants +2 AC and Advantage on ranged attacks.
* **Hydra Awakening:** More than 10 slashing/piercing damage in the sump awakens the Gore-Leech Hydra on Initiative 20.

### 3. Fort Kar-Vash (The Salt-Rot Citadel)
* **Architecture:** Stepped black volcanic basalt; iron gibbets holding bleached slave ribcages; sulfur steam vents.
* **Blood-Dowsing Braziers:** Emerald flames flare violent violet-crimson within 10 ft of human blood. Bypassed via ceiling rafters (DC 13 Acrobatics) or flue sabotage (DC 13 Sleight of Hand).

### 4. The Sunken Amphitheater of Bone
* **Magma Trench:** 10-ft-wide circular moat of bubbling molten slag (6d10 fire damage on entry; standing within 5 ft deals 1d6 fire at turn start).
* **Winch Cables (AC 14, 15 HP):** Can be struck or locked to halt the descent of the crosses.

## 👥 4. NPC PROFILES (STORMWRECK ISLE FRAMEWORK)

### Old Geth (One-Eyed Goblin Apothecary)
* **Philosophy:** *Quiet Compassion in a World of Iron.* Power without purpose is vulgar butchery.
* **Dilemma:** Comatose and dying human stock; secretly brewing painkilling salves while evading inspection.
* **Moral Stand (Red Line):** Refuses to assist in public child sacrifices; will aid those with the courage to fight back.
* **Voice:** *"You have demon horns, boy... but your eyes are haunted by human sorrow."*
* **Vitals:** AC 11 | HP 22 | Init +1 | Speed 25 ft | CR 1/4 | Saves: INT +4, WIS +4

### Elia (10-Year-Old Human Captive)
* **Philosophy:** *Innocence in the Ashes.* Believes someone strong will break the chains.
* **Dilemma:** Chained to central cross above magma; heat blistering her feet.
* **Voice:** *"You bleed like me... Are you an angel... or a demon?"*
* **Vitals:** AC 10 | HP 4 | Init +0 | Speed 25 ft | CR 0

### Grand Inquisitor Gortash (Saint of the Bleeding Cross)
* **Philosophy:** *The Bleeding Cross & Absolute Purge.* Human blood is an infectious blight; hybrids must be exterminated.
* **Dilemma:** Constructing the Aethel-Engine in Zul-Gaza; harvesting 10,000 human souls before the Blood Eclipse.
* **Voice:** *"Leave only ash. The Aethel-Engine will drink ten thousand souls."*
* **Vitals:** AC 19 | HP 175 | Init +3 | Speed 35 ft, fly 60 ft | Truesight 60 ft | CR 14

## ⚖️ 6. DM QUICK REFERENCE & DIFFICULTY DCS

### Table Difficulty DCs
* **DC 10 (Easy):** Spot obvious tracks, scale dry wooden wall, notice sulfur buildup.
* **DC 12 (Moderate):** Identify rare bog alchemy herbs, stomp mud over fresh crimson blood.
* **DC 13 (Challenging):** Sneak across Fort Kar-Vash ceiling rafters, pick simple slave collar lock.
* **DC 14 (Hard):** Sever amphitheater winch cable under fire, conceal red blood under brazier flare.
* **DC 18 (Very Hard):** Pick master volcanic iron shackles without key, recall Saint-tier demon weakness.
* **DC 20 (Heroic):** Snap hardened volcanic iron chains with bare hands, resist Inquisitor soul-aura.
