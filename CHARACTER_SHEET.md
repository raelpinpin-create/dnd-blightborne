## 🎲 BATTLE MASTER MANEUVERS (4 KNOWN • 4d8 SUPERIORITY POOL)
* **Trip Attack:** On weapon hit, expend 1 die to add **+1d8 damage**. Large or smaller target must succeed on a **DC 15 STR Save** or fall **Prone**.
* **Riposte:** When an enemy misses you with a melee attack, spend your **Reaction** and 1 die to strike back (+7 Hit); on hit, add **+1d8 damage**.
* **Precision Attack:** Expend 1 die to add **+1d8 to an attack roll** before or after seeing the roll.
* **Menacing Attack:** On weapon hit, expend 1 die to add **+1d8 damage**. Target must succeed on a **DC 15 WIS Save** or become **Frightened** of you until the end of your next turn.

## ⚡ FIGHTER CLASS FEATURES & FEATS
* **Extra Attack:** Attack twice per Attack action.
* **Action Surge (1/Short or Long Rest):** Gain 1 additional action on your turn.
* **Second Wind (2/Short or Long Rest):** Bonus Action to regain `1d10 + 5` HP.
* **Tactical Mind:** When failing an ability check, expend a Second Wind use to add `+1d10` to the roll; if you still fail, the use is refunded.
* **Fighting Style — Great Weapon Fighting:** When rolling damage with a two-handed melee weapon, any 1 or 2 counts as a 3.
* **Origin Feat (Soldier) — Savage Attacker:** Once per turn on a hit, roll the weapon's damage dice twice and keep the higher result.
* **Level 4 Feat — Great Weapon Master:** +1 STR; adds +3 PB damage once per turn; bonus action attack on crits/kills.
