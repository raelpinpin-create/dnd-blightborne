## 📜 PROLOGUE: THE ASHES OF SANCTUARY (YEAR 485 OF THE DEMON HEGEMONY)

* **The Hidden Commune:** 
  Fifteen years ago, hidden deep within the sulfuric borderland forests between Sector 09 and the wild mountains, lay **Sanctuary**—the only place in the entire Demon Hegemony where runaway human laborers and sympathetic demon dissidents lived in peaceful defiance of the imperial caste.
* **The Forbidden Child:** 
  Here, **Laxas Langley** was born—the illicit half-demon, half-human hybrid child of a human mother and a demon rebel. He possessed human bone structure, expressive features, and crimson red blood, blended with subtle obsidian brow ridges and glowing sulfur irises.
* **The Extermination Directive:** 
  When imperial inquisitorial scouts uncovered Sanctuary's coordinates, an ironclad military cohort swept down upon the village in the dead of night. 
  * The commander ruthlessly executed every elder and pushed a terrified 10-year-old child onto an iron spike before ordering the mass burning of the compound.
  * 42 demon sympathizers were crucified on blazing iron beams; 19 human laborers were executed by summary decapitation.
* **The Rebirth of Rashuk:** 
  10-year-old Laxas survived only by burying himself in the freezing, sulfuric mud beneath his collapsing family home, watching through the smoke as the inferno consumed everything he loved. Taking the demon name **Rashuk**, he spent the next 15 years in mercenary companies, frontline shock battalions, and gladiatorial arenas—forging his body and sword technique into an unbreakable killing machine.

## 🩸 SESSION 1 CHRONICLE: THE ASHEN TRAIL & THE FALL OF KAR-VASH

### 🌲 Act 1: The Dead-Water Gulch Ambush
* **The Incident at Convoy Sector 09:** 
  While serving as a mercenary guard on an imperial slave convoy, Rashuk witnessed Convoy Commander Zorath drag a 10-year-old human slave girl named **Elia** to the branding brazier. Seeing the ghost of his younger self and the murdered child from Sanctuary, Rashuk's restraint shattered. With a single devastating greatsword stroke, he decapitated Commander Zorath, seized the commander's locked brass dispatch satchel, and fled into the misty depths of the **Rot-Wood Bog**.
* **The Chasm Bottleneck:** 
  Pursued by the **Iron Fang Pack** (2 Bloodhound Chimeras, 2 Iron Fang Scouts, and the brutal Captain Krug), Rashuk lured his hunters into the narrow bottleneck of **Dead-Water Gulch**—a 20-foot basalt trench choked with knee-deep black mud.
* **Tactical Triumph & The Red Blood Secret:** 
  Utilizing superior positioning along the chasm ridge and heavy greatsword swings, Rashuk cleaved through the war-hounds and severed Captain Krug's spine. When taking a glancing strike, Rashuk swiftly performed a **DC 12 Athletics check** to stomp the mud and conceal his vibrant crimson human blood before the dying scouts could identify him as a hybrid.

---

### 🏰 Act 2: Infiltrating the Salt-Rot Citadel
* **The Infiltration of Fort Kar-Vash:** 
  Following the convoy trail to its origin, Rashuk scaled the black volcanic ramparts of **Fort Kar-Vash**, a massive basalt bastion rising above a sulfur rift.
* **The Emerald Braziers:** 
  Inside the corridors, Rashuk navigated past the garrison's **Blood-Dowsing Braziers**—magical cauldrons that flare violent violet-crimson in the presence of human blood—by stalking across the overhead roof rafters (DC 13 Acrobatics).
* **Meeting Old Geth:** 
  In the subterranean slave infirmary, Rashuk encountered **Old Geth**, a one-eyed, hunchbacked goblin apothecary who spent his days secretly brewing painkilling ghost-poppy salves for dying human stock. 
  * Sensing the deep human grief behind Rashuk's demonic eyes, Geth gifted him a flask of **Alchemical Quenching Dust** to suppress alarm braziers and warned him:  
    > *"Power without a purpose to protect, boy... just makes you another butcher in iron armor."*

---

### 🌋 Act 3: Duel in the Amphitheater of Bone (The 2-Turn Nova)
* **The Arena of Molten Slag:** 
  Sirens wailed as Overseer Vhol prepared to execute the remaining captive stock in the **Sunken Amphitheater of Bone**—a 60-foot gladiatorial pit ringed by bubbling orange magma. Suspended above the magma on high winch cables were three timber crosses, with young Elia chained to the central beam.
* **The 2-Turn Annihilation of Overseer Vhol:** 
  Breaching the arena gantry, Rashuk engaged **Overseer Vhol**, a four-armed demon centurion clad in spiked obsidian plate and armed with a massive executioner cleaver and boiling pitch boiler.
  * **Round 1:** Rashuk closed the distance, striking with terrifying precision. Expending a Superiority Die on a **Trip Attack** (DC 15 Str Save), Rashuk swept Vhol's legs out from under him, knocking the 9-foot centurion **Prone**. Rashuk followed through with Great Weapon Master power strikes, shredding through Vhol's first phase HP.
  * **Round 2 (Action Surge Nova):** Triggering **Action Surge**, Rashuk unleashed a furious four-attack barrage into the prone boss with Great Weapon Fighting rerolls and maximum lethality. Before Vhol's manic Phase 2 blood frenzy could even manifest or the magma winch could lower Elia into the lava, Rashuk drove his steel through Vhol's chest plate and severed the Overseer's head in a spray of black demon ichor.

---

### 💀 Act 4: The Retaliatory Massacre & The Lawful Evil Awakening
* **The Bitter Spoils:** 
  Standing over the centurion's corpse, Rashuk claimed the officer's legendary **Obsidian Greatcleaver** (`2d6+4 Slashing + Laceration`), the **Inquisitorial Signet Ring**, the **Obsidian Vault Master Key**, and the **Grand Tithe Supply Map to Zul-Gaza**.
* **The Fortress Alarm & The Infirmary Slaughter:** 
  As alarms blared across Fort Kar-Vash, Rashuk slipped into the drainage flues with the acquired intelligence. However, the enraged garrison sentries, finding their commander decapitated and assuming an internal slave revolt, launched an immediate extermination sweep across the slave pens.
  * **Old Geth**, **10-year-old Elia**, and every single human captive in the infirmary were systematically slaughtered by the demon garrison and hurled into the magma vents.
* **⚖️ The Alignment Transformation (Lawful Neutral ➔ LAWFUL EVIL):**
  * Watching the black smoke billow from the fortress towers into the night sky, the memory of the 10-year-old child killed during the Sanctuary purge resurfaced with overwhelming clarity.
  * Rashuk realized that in this brutal world, **mercy is a fatal disease, and innocence is merely kindling for imperial fires.**
  * His empathy hardened into impenetrable obsidian. Rashuk abandoned all sentimental delusions of heroism, embracing a cold, disciplined, and ruthless code of **Lawful Evil**:  
    > *He will not save the weak. He will not offer quarter. He will march down the Ashen Trail, hijack the imperial supply lines with the stolen signet ring, and burn the Inquisitors of Zul-Gaza until only ash remains.*

## 🗺️ CAMPAIGN ROADMAP & DISCOVERED INTEL

### 1. The Grand Tithe Supply Network (10,000 Souls for Zul-Gaza)
The parchment map recovered from Overseer Vhol's desk details the continental supply chain feeding souls to Grand Inquisitor Gortash's mysterious **Aethel-Engine**:
1. 💀 **Fort Kar-Vash (Sector 09):** Volcanic outpost and sulfur basin. *(Overseer Vhol slain; fortress left in chaotic disarray).*
2. ⛰️ **The Iron Mines of Oros (Sector 08):** Subterranean basalt quarry and soul-funnel processing 3,800 human laborers. *(Current Destination).*
3. 🏰 **The Sanguine Bastion (Sector 03):** Heavily fortified gateway bastion guarding the approach to the capital.
4. 👑 **Zul-Gaza (The Imperial Core):** Citadel of the High Inquisitors, site of the central Aethel-Engine.

### 2. Strategic Assets in Rashuk's Possession
* 🪓 **Overseer Vhol's Obsidian Greatcleaver:** Devastating two-handed greatweapon with the *Laceration* arterial bleed effect.
* 💍 **Inquisitorial Signet Ring:** Stamped with *A Crown of Bone Piercing a Weeping Eye*. Authorizes military transport requisitions and checkpoint bypass.
* 🔑 **Obsidian Vault Master Key:** Unlocks high-security gates, armory chests, and regional imperial lockboxes.
* 📦 **Locked Courier Brass Satchel:** Contains classified imperial directives linking back to the Sanctuary purge.
