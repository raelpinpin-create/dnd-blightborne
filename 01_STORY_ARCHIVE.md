## 📜 CAMPAIGN PROLOGUE: THE ASHES OF SANCTUARY (15 YEARS AGO)
* In the year 485 of the Demon Hegemony, a hidden borderland village known as *Sanctuary* was betrayed and reported to the imperial inquisition for harboring runaway human slaves and the birth of a forbidden half-demon hybrid.
* Imperial forces led by **Grand Inquisitor Gortash** razed the village to the ground. 42 demon sympathizers were crucified and set ablaze; 19 human laborers were exterminated on site.
* The 10-year-old hybrid, **Laxas Langley**, hid in the burning mud, watching his parents die. He survived, adopted the demon name **Rashuk**, and spent 15 years fighting as a ruthless mercenary to achieve unbreakable strength.
