## 📜 CAMPAIGN PROLOGUE: THE ASHES OF SANCTUARY (15 YEARS AGO)
* In the year 485 of the Demon Hegemony, a hidden borderland village known as *Sanctuary* was betrayed and reported to the imperial inquisition for harboring runaway human slaves and the birth of a forbidden half-demon hybrid.
* Imperial inquisition forces razed the village to the ground. 42 demon sympathizers were crucified and set ablaze; 19 human laborers were exterminated on site.
* The 10-year-old hybrid, **Laxas Langley**, hid in the burning mud, watching his parents die. He survived, adopted the demon name **Rashuk**, and spent 15 years fighting as a ruthless mercenary to achieve unbreakable strength.

---

## 🩸 SESSION 1: THE ASHEN TRAIL & THE MASSACRE OF KAR-VASH
* **Act 1 — The Bloodhound Ambush:** After executing Convoy Commander Zorath, Rashuk led the Iron Fang pack into a bottleneck at Dead-Water Gulch. He eliminated Captain Krug and the chimera hounds, successfully concealing his crimson human blood in the acidic swamp slurry.
* **Act 2 — Salt-Rot Citadel Infiltration:** Rashuk scaled the basalt ramparts of Fort Kar-Vash, bypassed the emerald Blood-Dowsing Braziers, and infiltrated the lower slave infirmary, discovering the locked courier satchel and securing emergency alchemy from Old Geth.
* **Act 3 — Duel in the Amphitheater of Bone:** When war horns sounded the execution of the slave stock, Rashuk breached the arena gantry. In a visceral, high-stakes duel across the magma rim, Rashuk overpowered **Overseer Vhol**, cleaving through the four-armed centurion and executing him in cold blood.
* **Act 4 — The Bitter Escape & The Price of Ash:** 
  * Rather than lingering to defend the defenseless, Rashuk claimed Vhol's heavy **Obsidian Greatcleaver**, the **Inquisitorial Signet Ring**, the **Vault Master Key**, and the **Grand Tithe Supply Map to Zul-Gaza**, slipping into the sulfur flues as fortress sirens wailed.
  * **The Retaliation:** When the demon garrison discovered Overseer Vhol dead, they blamed an internal slave uprising. Sentries swept the infirmary: **Old Geth**, **10-year-old Elia**, and all human captives were massacred and cast into the magma pit.
* **⚖️ Moral Transformation (Lawful Neutral ➔ LAWFUL EVIL):**
  * Watching the smoke rise over Fort Kar-Vash and remembering the screams of Sanctuary, Rashuk's lingering empathy hardened into obsidian.
  * He resolved that mercy in this world is a lethal delusion. Embracing a cold, ruthless code of absolute power and calculating retribution, Rashuk steps onto the ashen road to Zul-Gaza: *he will dismantle the Hegemony hierarchy not to save the weak, but to bury his enemies in ash.*
