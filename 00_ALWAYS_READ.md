## ⚡ TABLE RULES CHEAT-SHEET
* **Combat Initiation:** Everyone rolls Initiative immediately before any declared attack resolves.
* **Ability Checks in Combat:** Consumes full Action.
* **Help Tokens:** 2/2 max per PC. +1d6 Advantage Die on d20 roll with valid narrative explanation.
* **0 HP & Death Saves:** DC 10 save on turn. 10+ = stabilize with 1 HP (Prone). 1–9 = 1 Strike. **2 Strikes = Permanent Death**. Taking damage 2 separate times while at 0 HP = permanent death.
* **Brutal Crits:** Nat 20 = Max Base Damage + 1 Normal Roll + Modifiers.
* **Blight-Born Blood Hazard:** Red human blood requires DC 12–14 concealment check when exposed.
* **Simplified Economy:** 10 SP = 1 GP. No CP.
