## ⚖️ 3. DM COMBAT BALANCING & HP SCALING NOTES (SOLO FIGHTER NOVA TUNING)

> [!TIP]
> **Post-Session 1 Combat Analysis:**
> Rashuk's Level 5 Battle Master build outputs massive burst damage (2 Attacks/Turn + Action Surge = 4 Greatweapon attacks + Superiority Dice + Great Weapon Master `+3` + GWF rerolls), easily dealing **45–65+ damage in a single round**. Overseer Vhol (85 total HP across 2 phases) was decimated in just 2 turns.
> 
> **Balancing Guidelines for Act 2 (The Iron Mines of Oros & Beyond):**
> 1. **Boss HP Calibration:** Future solo bosses (such as Centurion Taskmasters or Inquisitorial Enforcers) should be scaled to **120–150+ HP** (or structured with strict Multi-Phase Armor Plates / Damage Gates) so encounters last a satisfying 3–4 tactical rounds against nova burst rounds.
> 2. **Minion Action Economy & Cover:** Include 2–3 tactical ranged skirmishers on elevated chokepoints or terrain hazards (sulfur vents, magma seams) to force Rashuk to divide his attacks rather than focus-firing all 4 strikes into a single boss on Round 1.
> 3. **Defensive Reactions:** Equip elite enemy officers with *Parry* (+3 AC on melee reaction), *Shield Wall*, or *Uncanny Dodge* to absorb one heavy greatcleaver blow per round.

## ⚡ 6. MASTER TABLE RULES QUICK REFERENCE

* **Combat Initiation:** Everyone rolls Initiative immediately BEFORE any declared attack resolves. Zero free pre-combat attacks.
* **Ability Checks in Combat:** Active checks (Perception, Insight, Investigation) consume your **Full Action**.
* **Help Tokens:** 2/2 max per character. Spend 1 token to give an ally within 30 ft a **+1d6 Advantage Die** with narrative explanation.
* **0 HP & Death Saves (2-Strike Lethal System):** DC 10 save on turn. 10+ = stabilize with 1 HP (Prone). 1–9 = 1 Strike. **2 Strikes = Permanent Death**. Taking damage 2 separate times at 0 HP = instant death.
* **Brutal Crits:** On Nat 20 = **Maximum Base Damage + 1 Normal Damage Roll + Ability Modifiers**.
* **Blight-Born Blood Hazard:** Rashuk's crimson human blood requires a **DC 12–14 check** to wipe away or conceal when exposed near demon sniffers or dowsing braziers.
* **Simplified Economy:** 10 Silver Pieces (SP) = 1 Gold Piece (GP). No copper exists.
